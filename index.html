Hi, I'm Mara.

I'm a full-time designer who picked up a camera five years ago and never quite put it down. What started as documenting weekend hikes turned into a way of processing the world — of noticing the steam off a morning coffee, the geometry of a rain puddle, the way light bends through a window.

This blog is my visual diary. No perfection, no pressure — just honest photographs and the thoughts that come with them.

"The camera is a tool for seeing. It teaches you to look before you click, and to wait for the moment that deserves to be kept."
